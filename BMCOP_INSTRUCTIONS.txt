COIL ON PLUG Script V3.X for Pico2 (RP2350) made by Bouletmarc (BMDevs)

The Coil On Plugs Adapter is intended to be used on Honda/Acura 4cylinders
cars running on a distributor normally. (Mostly Serie B-D-F-H Engines)

This application samples require Three Signals wires (ICM, FC1 Pin3 & FC1 Pin4)
It activate 4x Digital ouputs pins (used to trigger each coils)
depending on which cylinder should fire.

***********************************************************************/
***********************************************************************/
***********************************************************************/
REQUIREMENTS:
***********************************************************************/
-1x BMCOP V3 Adapter Kit
-4x Coils Pack (from K20/K24?)
-Couple Wires (atleast 4x that cover the distance between the ECU and the Coils where they will be installed on the engine.
-1x OBD1 ECU
-Soldering Skills


***********************************************************************/
INTALLATION:
***********************************************************************/
1. Remove the ECU from the vehicle and open the case to solders the parts

2. Remove the Solders at FC1 locations and solder the 12pins male header
(If the ecu has a Knock Board installed, you must removed the board and install the 12pins male header)

3. Solder the 4pins (2x2) male header on the BMCOP adapter on the side you prefer
(if you prefer the 4pins header on the back or the front of the BMCOP adapter)
->If required drill a small hole in the ecu case where the 4x coils wire and Tach signal will pass thru.

4. Disconnect the ICM signal from the Distributor and Connect it with the BMCOP
->On the ecu connectors, cut the wire at A21 (ICM) and leave a couple inches (~6") free so you can reconnect the wire later
  if you want to run again on the distributor
->Connect A21 with the BMCop, you can choose between: 
  -soldering A21 wire on the ECU connectors Pins inside the ECU
  -Use the A21 wire cut outside on the ECU connectors and connect it with the BMCOP
->Keep disconnected the A21 wire going into the firewall (going to the distributor)

5. Disconnect the 12V Power Source on the Distributor (Black/Yel) wire so the Distributor Coil is not powered

6. Connect A25 (12V) with the BMCOP 
(Solder the wire on A25 pin inside the ecu or tap the wire with A25 outside the ecu)

7. Connect the Tach Signal (RPM) with the BMCOP
(Connecting the tach signal can be a little tricky and differ for some cars models)
->On most 88-98 models, the Tach signal is the blue wire on the Distributor Connector
  -You need to disconnect/cut the blue wire going to the distributor and connect the blue wire
   thats on the engine harness side with the BMCOP
->On some 99-00 models, the Tach signal is tapped/splitted with the ICM signal near the ecu connectors, close to the firewall
  -If you find where the tap/split is with the icm wire, cut the wire from being connected with ICM wire and
   connect the wire with the BMCOP
->If you dont find the tach signal wire or spend time on searching, located in the engine bay at the shock tower on the driver side
  you can find a single blue wire connector thats not connected to anything generally, Connect a wire from this blue wire
  to the Tach RPM Signal on the BMCOP
  
8. Remove the Spark Plugs wires completly and install the Coils Pack
-> You'll need to bring 12v and Ground to each Coils Pack
   -For a protected circuit, its recommanded to put a 10A fuse on the 12V wire that power all the coils
   -Connect the 12V wire that power the coils with an 12V Ignition (Key 'ON' only, not a 12V accessory)
-> Connect each Coils Pack Trigger Wire to the appropriate Coil Pin on the BMCOP

*NOT REQUIRED* Remove the Coil and Ignition Control Module inside the Distributor
-> This step in not required for testing the product, however if the product works correctly and you
   want a permanent installation, it might be recommanded to pull out the Coil and Ignition Control Module 
   inside the distributor. The Cap Rotor can also be removed safely.
   

***********************************************************************/
TESTING THE PRODUCTS AND AVAILABLES OPTIONS:
***********************************************************************/
The BMCOP is ready to be tested upon receiving it. When the installation is done, just turn the Key ON and give it a try!

The BMCOP come with 2x custom features for my Testers to try and inform me whats better 
to be used as default on the final version. (Public production version)

->Wasted Spark(WS) Feature: If enabled it fire 2x concecutives cylinders at the same time (1-4) and (2-3) fires in pair.
							Else it fire individually the cylinders (1-3-4-2)
						
->Fast Sync(FS) Feature: If enabled it will start firing the coils slightly earlier when starting the engine and wont wait
						on a full crank revolution to start firing them. This is for Testing purpose to exactly know if
						this feature can be enabled without causing issues. If it doesn't work correctly, the coils will
						fire on the wrong cylinders (unsynced) for the first crank revolution. Testers has to report if 
						it's safe to enable this feature and keep it enabled for the final version.
					 
The BMCOP has Wasted Spark(WS) and Fast Sync(FS) DISABLED by default when you receive them.

4x Files come with the BMCOP for testing purpose:
-BMCopV3_NO_WS_NO_FS.uf2 		(Default file, wasted spark disabled and fast sync disabled)
-BMCopV3_NO_WS_WITH_FS.uf2		(wasted spark disabled and fast sync enabled)
-BMCopV3_WITH_WS_NO_FS.uf2		(wasted spark enabled and fast sync disabled)
-BMCopV3_WITH_WS_WITH_FS.uf2	(wasted spark enabled and fast sync enabled)


***********************************************************************/
HOW TO CHANGE THE PROGRAM CODE FILE ON THE BMCOP:
***********************************************************************/
You can notice 5x tiny holes aligned on the BMCOP board with 'DIAG' near the holes and that 2x of the holes are encircled,
you'll need to touch/connect the 2x holes together to put the BMCOP into 'Boot Mode'

->Use the tiny metal pins I gave with the BMCop, insert them in the 2x holes and make the 2x pins touch themselves for easier Boot Mode

TO ENTER BOOT MODE:
-At first, make sure the USB is disconnected, is the BMCOP is still in the vehicule, make sure the Ignition Key is OFF and 
 the ecu is not be powered or remove the the BMCOP from the ecu the time you update the firmware.
-Make sure the 2x pins/holes of DIAG on the BMCOP are connected together
-Connect a USB-C cable to the BMCOP with your computer
->If the 2x pins are NOT correctly touching/connected together, nothing will happens on your computer, no windows are showing up
  -In that case just disconnect the USB, make sure again that the 2 pins are connected together and repeat (reconnect USB, etc)
  -You can hold the two pins with your fingers when plugging the USB
->If the 2x pins are correctly touching/connected together, you'll see a windows pop upon connecting the USB cable on your
  computer just like you would have connected a USB memory stick, SD Card or something similar.
-Drag and Drop the XXXX.uf2 file directly into the BMCOP and the BMCOP will reboot itself 2sec after.
-You can disconnect the 2pins before dropping the .uf2 file so that when the BMCOP reboot 2sec after dropping the .uf2 is doesn't 
restart in 'Boot Mode' and confuse you.
